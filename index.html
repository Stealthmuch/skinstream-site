SkinStream

A skincare routine tracker that actually watches for ingredient conflicts.

In development — not yet on Google Play

SkinStream helps you build and stick to a morning, daytime, and night skincare routine — scan a product's barcode or its label to add it, and SkinStream tracks what you're using, reminds you when it's time, and flags real interaction risks (like stacking too many actives, or exfoliating and using a retinoid the same day) before they irritate your skin.

Barcode & label scanning — add products by scanning a barcode or photographing the ingredient list; no manual typing needed.
Ingredient interaction warnings — sourced guidance on which actives shouldn't be combined the same day.
Rotation-aware routine builder — multi-week rotations, not just a flat 7-day repeat, built around your preferences and skin conditions.
Barrier mode — one tap switches your whole routine to gentle, barrier-repair-appropriate products when your skin needs a break.
Routine gap suggestions — if your routine is missing a basic (no morning sunscreen, no cleanser, or an active with no moisturiser), SkinStream points it out and suggests products that fit your preferences, such as fragrance-free or avoiding ingredients you've excluded.
Reminders & streaks — per-routine reminders, sunscreen reapplication nudges, and completion tracking so routines actually stick.

How SkinStream is funded

SkinStream will be free. In future, some product suggestions may include affiliate links to skincare retailers, so we may earn a commission if you buy through one, at no extra cost to you. Every affiliate link will be clearly labelled in the app. Suggestions are based on your routine, ingredients and preferences, never on which retailer pays a commission. SkinStream has no ads and doesn't sell your data.

Who's behind it

SkinStream is developed and operated by Erick, an independent developer based in Melbourne, Australia, as part of the same small app suite as PackStream.
